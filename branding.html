Structural OOH & Large Format

Outdoor Communication & Billboard Engineering

In high-speed urban transit corridors, a billboard has less than 2.8 seconds to establish recognition. That requires profound architectural discipline: calculation of motorists' viewing cones, vertical declination angles, typographic scale, and chromatic contrast against changing daylight conditions.

✓
Sightline & Speed-Scale Analysis

Typography and focal imagery sized proportionally to viewing distance (50m to 300m) and approach velocity, preventing visual clutter.

✓
Wind-Load & Sub-Structure Safety

Structural MS truss verification engineered to withstand monsoon gusts, with anti-tear perimeter grommets and tensioned cable anchors.

✓
Pigment & UV Substrate Durability

Printed with industrial solvent and UV-cured ink sets on calibrated heavy-duty flex, preventing color fade and chalking in Dhaka's intense sun.

Perimeter Spatial Branding

Project Fencing & Boundary Hoardings

Real estate developers frequently lose brand prestige because their project boundary fences wrinkle, sag, or tear within weeks of installation. At Innerloop Atelier, we treat fencing as a temporary architectural facade.

✓
Precision Box-Pipe Perimeter Framing

We construct rigid MS box-pipe substructures with diagonal cross-bracing to prevent wind deflection and warping along long site boundaries.

✓
Anti-Wrinkle Tensioning & Flush Seams

Continuous mechanical tensioning with heavy-gauge turnbuckles ensures the graphic skin remains drum-tight across hundreds of feet without unsightly sagging.

✓
Featured Real Project: The Premium Homes — Canvas of Happiness

Comprehensive boundary fencing spanning the entire site boundary, maintaining strict proportion, typography contrast, and visual rhythm across streetscapes.

The Execution Discipline

From First Sightline Audit to Final Site Sign-Off.

Every project moves through five rigorous quality gates before delivery.

01

Site & Velocity Audit

Measurement of viewing distances, approach speeds, sun trajectories, and civil mounting points.

02

Architectural CAD & 3D

Elevation drawings, lux illumination simulations, and proportioned typographic scaling.

03

Material & BOQ Spec

Definite structural metal thickness, LED wattage, UV ink profiles, and structural safety margins.

04

In-House Fabrication

CNC cutting, welded box-pipe frames, acrylic bending, and optical diffusion testing.

05

Night & Day Handover

On-site installation with zero sagging, daylight readability verification, and electrical sign-off.
