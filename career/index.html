Architecture & Spatial ● Actively Hiring Fresh Graduate / Experienced Full-Time · Dhaka

Architect (Fresh Graduate / Experienced)

Shape high-precision commercial offices, luxury residences, functional kitchens, and retail showrooms — from spatial layout and lighting calibration to live site execution.

Ask HR ↗

Key Responsibilities

1 Interior Design

Spatial planning, ergonomic workstation & residential layouts, custom joinery & kitchen detailing, photorealistic 3D modeling, and BNBC-compliant execution drawings.

2 Lighting Architecture

Layered ambient, task, and accent lighting schedules — calibrating 2700K–4000K color temperatures, cove/shadow-gap ceiling details, and high-CRI (90+) retail & hospitality fixtures.

3 Materials & Execution Knowledge

Specifying BWR marine ply, veneers, quartz, and acoustic finishes; coordinating MEP routing, BOQ quantity take-offs, and supervising site execution through 50-point snagging.

Ideal Profile & Toolkit

  • B.Arch degree — welcoming sharp Fresh Graduates and experienced Architects alike.
  • Strong grasp of architectural proportion, joinery tolerances, and real-site constructability.
  • Confidence visiting active fitout sites in Dhaka to inspect work with our execution crew.
AutoCAD SketchUp / Revit 3ds Max / V-Ray Lumion / Enscape BOQ & MEP Coordination
Brand & Print Craft ● Actively Hiring Fine Arts + Print Experience Full-Time · Dhaka

Graphic Designer (Fine Arts Background with Print Design Experience)

Lead visual craftsmanship across brand identity systems, commercial offset print, packaging, and large-format environmental signage with classical artistic rigor.

Ask HR ↗

Key Responsibilities

1 Fine Arts Visual Craft

Apply strong artistic fundamentals — visual hierarchy, hand-drawn sensitivity, color harmony, and bespoke typography — across brand guidelines and flagship campaigns.

2 Print Design & Prepress

Prepare press-ready mechanicals with accurate CMYK separation, bleed/trim tolerances, die-lines, spot UV/foil plates, paper GSM selection, and offset/CTP quality checks.

3 Spatial & Signage Graphics

Design massive real-estate perimeter fencing, 3D illuminated acrylic/SS signage layouts, exhibition stalls, and corporate print collateral alongside our spatial architects.

Ideal Profile & Toolkit

  • Fine Arts academic background (BFA / MFA or equivalent conservatory training).
  • Hands-on commercial print design experience — books, brochures, packaging, or outdoor print.
  • Portfolio demonstrating original visual craft, layout discipline, and finished print outputs.
Adobe Illustrator Adobe InDesign Adobe Photoshop CMYK & Prepress Typography & Illustration
