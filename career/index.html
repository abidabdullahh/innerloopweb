Full-Time · Fresh Graduate / Experienced · Dhaka

Architect

Work across commercial offices, residential interiors, kitchens, and retail showrooms from concept layout to site execution.

Key Responsibilities

  1. 1. Interior Design — Space planning, functional layouts, 3D modeling, bespoke joinery detailing, and working drawings.
  2. 2. Lighting Architecture — Layered ambient, task, and accent lighting schedules, lux planning, cove & ceiling details, and fixture selection.
  3. 3. Materials & Execution Knowledge — Material specification (BWR ply, veneers, stone, acoustic finishes), BOQ coordination, and on-site execution supervision.

Preferred: B.Arch degree (Fresh Graduate or experienced), proficiency in AutoCAD, SketchUp/Revit, and 3D rendering tools.

Full-Time · Fine Arts & Print Experience · Dhaka

Graphic Designer

Lead visual identity, print production, and brand communication collateral with strong fine arts fundamentals.

Key Responsibilities

  1. 1. Fine Arts Foundation — Strong sense of form, composition, color harmony, illustration, and typographic hierarchy.
  2. 2. Print Design & Prepress — Production-ready artwork (CMYK, bleed/trim, die-lines, paper stock & offset/CTP coordination) for brochures, calendars, and packaging.
  3. 3. Brand & Signage Collateral — Visual identity assets, event backdrops, outdoor branding, and environmental signage graphics.

Preferred: Fine Arts background (BFA / MFA or equivalent), hands-on print design experience, and fluency in Illustrator, InDesign, and Photoshop.
