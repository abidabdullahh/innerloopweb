### How to Deploy the Fitout Delivery OS
1. Import `Innerloop_Fitout_Delivery_OS.csv` into **Google Sheets**, **Microsoft Excel**, or **Notion** (via Import CSV -> Database).
2. **Module 1 (BOQ Engine):** Replace sample quantities with your project takeoff measurements. All formulas calculate direct costs and automatic margin markups (default 18%).
3. **Module 2 (8-Week Gantt):** Link tasks to your site supervisor or subcontractor teams. Update statuses weekly.
4. **Module 3 (Material Spec Matrix):** Print and attach to subcontractor contracts to prevent material substitution on site.
5. **Module 4 (50-Point Snagging Protocol):** Perform site audits with client/contractor 5 days before final handover.

---

### Key Formulas Included:
* Direct Material + Labor Cost: `=Quantity * (Material_Rate + Labor_Rate)`
* Selling Price with Markup: `=Direct_Cost * (1 + Markup_Percentage)`
* Contingency Reserve Buffer: 5% automatically recommended for MEP & concealed renovations.
