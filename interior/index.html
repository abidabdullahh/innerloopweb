Discipline 03 · Interior Architecture

Interior design with architectural clarity.

Our interior practice is built around proportion, lighting calibration, and material durability — across corporate workstations, bespoke living wall units, bedrooms with layered ceilings, functional kitchens, retail showrooms, and restaurants.

Planning: BNBC-compliant layouts
Substrates: 18mm BWR marine ply
Lighting: 2700K–4000K lux calibration
Delivery: Joinery, MEP & turnkey fitout
Corporate Architecture & Ergonomics

Office Workstations & Commercial Headquarters

Modern workplaces must balance high-density efficiency with acoustic comfort and biophilic wellbeing. We design modular workstation systems with concealed wire trunking, acoustic fabric dividers, and ergonomic desk depths that eliminate clutter and foster focus.

✓
Concealed Wire Trunking & Raceway Geometry

Integrated under-desk power and Cat-6 data raceways with flip-up soft-touch access boxes, preventing cable hazards across team clusters.

✓
Acoustic Zoning & Focus Pods

High-density acoustic PET felt screens and perforated ceiling baffles to lower ambient sound reverberation (RT60 < 0.6s) across open-plan floors.

✓
Featured Real Project: Office Space — Dhanmondi

Full-floor corporate layout with executive cabins, linear team benches, architectural linear lighting, and collaborative breakout zones.

Joinery Craft & Media Engineering

Residential TV Cabinet & Living Wall Units

A living room focal wall requires delicate proportion between technology and architectural warmth. We design custom media consoles and feature walls that conceal every wire, provide AV equipment ventilation, and introduce tactile richness through fluted louvers and ambient backlighting.

✓
Concealed Conduits & Sub-Surface Wiring

In-wall PVC conduit channels for zero-visible wires between wall-mounted screens, soundbars, gaming consoles, and power outlets.

✓
Thermal Heat Dissipation for AV Equipment

Concealed ventilation louvers and perforated rear panels preventing heat buildup around receivers, set-top boxes, and consoles.

✓
Featured Real Project: Residential Interior — Uttara

Multi-functional living wall unit pairing matte charcoal fluted panels, warm oak veneer, indirect 3000K LED halo lighting, and push-to-open seamless storage.

Private Suites & Ceiling Architecture

Luxury Bedroom with False Ceiling & Layered Lighting

A master bedroom is a sanctuary where spatial acoustics and lighting temperatures govern tranquility. We build multi-tiered false ceilings with architectural shadow gaps, warm indirect cove illumination, and acoustic headboard paneling that elevates luxury into lived comfort.

✓
Architectural Ceiling Drops & Shadow Gaps

Clean 15mm perimeter shadow reveals with moisture-resistant gypsum boards and concealed aluminum framing, avoiding bulky stepped cornices.

✓
Dual-Layer Circadian Lighting (2700K - 3000K)

Diffused warm cove lighting paired with anti-glare deep-recessed spotlights (CRI > 92), creating restful evening moods without direct eye glare.

✓
Integrated Wardrobe & Fluted Headboard Walls

Floor-to-ceiling tinted glass or veneer wardrobes with internal sensor lighting and acoustically padded headboard backdrop walls.

Ergonomic Culinary Engineering

Premium & Functional Kitchen Interior Design

The kitchen is the hardest-working space in any residence. We apply the ergonomic "Golden Work Triangle" (refrigeration, prep/wash, cooking) engineered with marine-grade boiling-water-resistant (BWR) ply carcasses, soft-close hardware, and heat-resistant quartz surfaces.

✓
BWR Marine Ply & Anti-Moisture Substrates

Zero MDF in high-humidity zones. Carcasses built strictly from high-density 18mm BWR ply with 1mm edge banding to prevent water swelling.

✓
Austrian Blum Soft-Close Mechanisms

Tandembox pull-out drawers, corner carousel units, and Aventos lift-up overhead shutters tested for 100,000+ open-close cycles.

✓
Featured Real Project: Modern Kitchen — Lalmatia

Handle-less profile shutters, heat-resistant quartz stone countertop with under-mount stainless sink, and seamless built-in oven/hob integration.

The Execution Discipline

From 3D Laser Survey to Turnkey Handover.

Every interior fitout is governed by a meticulous 5-stage architectural pipeline.

01

3D Laser Site Survey

Millimeter-accurate digital as-built measurements of columns, beams, floor levels, and MEP service shafts.

02

Spatial Layout & BNBC

Ergonomic circulation plans, fire escape clearances, and room zoning compliant with Bangladesh building codes.

03

3D Renders & Spec Matrix

Photorealistic 3D visualizations, material sample boards, lighting schedules, and hardware specifications.

04

Turnkey Site Execution

In-house carpentry, MEP first/second fix, false ceilings, glass partitions, and specialized paint finishes.

05

50-Point Snagging & Handover

Rigorous defect audit checking drawer glides, lighting drivers, surface flatness, and deep clean handover.
