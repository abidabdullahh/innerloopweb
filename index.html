Abid Abdullah

Founder & Proprietor

Abid Abdullah is the founder of Innerloop Atelier, a practice focused on Brand Communication, Landscape Design, and Interior Design. His work reflects a practical understanding of branding, business, and presentation, shaped by years of hands-on experience.

He holds a BBA and an MBA in Management from National University. He began working in the branding sector in 2017, and since then he has developed a strong professional background in brand development, communication, and strategic positioning.

In 2017, he also started Rapid Drops Design, which marked the beginning of his independent journey in the field. Over time, he has continued to build experience through work that demands clarity, consistency, and a thoughtful approach to brand perception.

Through Innerloop Atelier, Abid Abdullah brings together experience, strategy, and creative direction in a way that feels grounded, professional, and purposeful.
